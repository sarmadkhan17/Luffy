# SELL signals underperform BUY signals

In the paired outcome set, BUY signals are right 20/31 (64.5%) while SELL signals are right only 6/49 (12.2%), indicating a strong directional asymmetry where sell-side signals are near-random-to-wrong; sample sizes are modest and the outcome table (n=80) does not perfectly match the regime table (n=82).
