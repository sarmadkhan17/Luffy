# Short gate is weakly supported by 4h outcome data

4h outcome direction shows SELL accuracy of 8/53 (15.1%) and BUY accuracy of 37/76 (48.7%); the TRENDING_DOWN SELL bucket (n=41) remains gated as a risk rule, but this is not a validated edge until fresh SELL signals clear a pre-registered accuracy threshold.
