# Funding Price Divergence

A long strategy that enters when price makes a new 192-bar Donchian high while 1920-bar funding z-score is below zero, filtered by ADX(14) > 18, with ATR(2.5) stop, 3.0 RR target, no trail, and max 64 bars. The thesis is that new highs with cooling funding indicate a non-leveraged, healthier rally that tends to persist.
