# BB 2.5σ Band Fade

A close back inside 2.5-sigma Bollinger bands after piercing them marks a failed extension: trapped late entrants unwind and price reverts toward the mean. Mid-width bands fire roughly twice as often as 3-sigma while keeping a modest positive expectancy. Intraday holds only (<=8h).
