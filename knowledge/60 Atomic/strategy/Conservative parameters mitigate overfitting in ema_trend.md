# Conservative parameters mitigate overfitting in ema_trend

Setting conservative parameters (adx_min=20.0, pullback_atr=0.6, trend_tf=15m) in the ema_trend strategy reduces overfitting by avoiding noise and focusing on stable trends with moderate pullback entries.
