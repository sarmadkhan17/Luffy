# High-signal-frequency setups escape the INSUFFICIENT regime

Prioritizing shorter-horizon intraday trend and mean-reversion candidates on liquid perpetuals, with explicit stop and max-drawdown parameters and stated win-rate priors, generates measurable trade flow within days rather than weeks, allowing evidence-gathering to proceed.
