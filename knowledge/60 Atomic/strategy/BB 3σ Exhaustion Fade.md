# BB 3σ Exhaustion Fade

A close back inside a 3-sigma Bollinger band after piercing it marks an exhausted counter-move: late entrants are trapped and price snaps back toward the mean. Wide 3σ bands filter noise so only genuine exhaustion events fire, in every regime. Intraday holds only (<=8h).
