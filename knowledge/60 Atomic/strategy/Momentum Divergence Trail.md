# Momentum Divergence Trail

In RANGING regimes, when price makes an extreme low/high but RSI momentum over the same 96-bar window refuses to confirm, passive/lagging flow is absorbing the last move; entering on this divergence with an ATR stop (2.5x), trailing exit armed at 1R (3x ATR), ADX<25 and rel_volume>1.0 filters, and max 200 bars aims to capture a snap fueled by late trend chasers.
