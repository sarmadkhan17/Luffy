# No multi-trade strategy family is profitable in the 30-trade window

In the 30-trade proving window (13 wins, -$891.20), every strategy family with n>=2 is negative: sweep_reversal 18/9/-$395.76, ema_trend family 9/3/-$372.66, rsi_extreme 2/0/-$124.17. The single BB 2.5σ win (+$1.39) is n=1 and does not soften these base rates. Loss attribution should use whole families, not just the largest variant.
