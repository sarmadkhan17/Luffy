# Seeding multiple hypothesis-based strategies without proven edge yields negative portfolio PnL

Seeding multiple hypothesis-based strategies that have not demonstrated a proven positive edge results in negative aggregate live PnL, low win rate, and poor profit factors, indicating the approach itself is flawed rather than needing incremental tweaks.
