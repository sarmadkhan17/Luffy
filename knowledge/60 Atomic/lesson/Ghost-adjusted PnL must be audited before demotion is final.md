# Ghost-adjusted PnL must be audited before demotion is final

When reconciled_ghost closes are present, subtracting only the visible ghost rows from a strategy's total yields a provisional adjusted PnL; the demotion is not fully supported until every trade's close_reason in the population is audited, because the dataset may not prove the visible ghost closes are the only ones.
