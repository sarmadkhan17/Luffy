# Paper trading data requirement before promotion

Strategies should accumulate sufficient paper trading data before being promoted to live, because most paper strategies in this portfolio lacked enough trade data to evaluate and the promoted live strategies (sweeprev, ematrend) accumulated significant negative PnL.
