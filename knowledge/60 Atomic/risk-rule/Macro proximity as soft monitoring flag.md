# Macro proximity as soft monitoring flag

Macro-event exposure should remain a monitoring flag rather than a hard entry gate until a controlled sample shows event-proximal trades underperform non-event trades, since the current journal has only 2 macro-tagged closes out of 12 recent trades, not the 5/12 previously cited.
