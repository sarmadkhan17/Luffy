# MacroGuard freezes entries around high-impact US events

Pre-fetching the Finnhub economic calendar and freezing entries 30 minutes before and 2 hours after any FOMC/CPI/NFP/Fed-speech event, with auto-resume when the window closes, prevents entries into scheduled macro shocks.
