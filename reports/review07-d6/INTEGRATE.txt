CONTRACT CHANGES THE REVIEWER SHOULD EXPECT
  1. Replay window: a FINAL, VALID receipt is replay-eligible if available_at_ms <=
     event + 2*tf (one bar after close). Exact-close-only is gone; later receipts remain
     retrospective. Receipt clocks are never changed.
  2. Replay takes each bar's FIRST eligible receipt. A later correction cannot reach the signal
     it post-dates (previously the last revision within the cut).
  3. Fill: open of the first retained bar starting at or after max(close, availability), within
     one bar of the decision. Signal exits use the same rule. max_bars exits stay at the final
     held bar's close (a clock event, not a data decision).
  4. Frames with receipt columns also refuse signals whose preceding WARMUP bars contain a
     missing bar (history_gap). Explicit retrospective datasets keep their declared contract
     (decide at close, labelled retrospective_observation / factory_validated=False) and do not
     gain this refusal.
  5. Evidence identity changes: engine_digest and the retrospective source hashes include
     fill_timing, so any pre-D6 run identity will (correctly) not match a D6 recomputation.
     Immutable stored evidence is untouched.

FINAL TIMING CONTRACT CORRECTION (follows commit 490d6d8)
  * Universal one-bar expiry REMOVED. StrategySpec declares no entry validity, so the existing
    freshness budget is used: interval + TTL after the signal close (market_provenance.DEFAULT_TTL_S,
    freshness_ms; the feed and kernel's usable_current age). Measured at the fill. MISSING CONTRACT:
    a per-strategy entry validity / TTL does not exist (Sol1 reported the same); add it to
    StrategySpec only if the owner wants strategy-specific expiry.
  * Receipt eligibility (replay_deadline_ms) = close + interval + TTL: a receipt later than the
    budget could never have been acted on and stays retrospective. It admits INPUTS only.
  * A decision waits for max availability over its lookback; refuses availability_unknown,
    signal_stale, no_executable_bar, unexecutable_price, history_gap. fill_gap/signal_expired merged
    into signal_stale (a one-bar-late price from a missing bar is allowed, two is not).
  * Lookback is per strategy (fill_timing.lookback_bars, bound to the frame as
    attrs['signal_lookback_bars'] by compile._ctx, capped at the existing WARMUP, minimum 15 for
    ATR(14) sizing). The global 210-bar gap rule is gone. WARMUP=210 (scoring floor) and the 400-bar
    loader floor in spec_evidence.load_frames are pre-existing, unchanged.
  * D7 merge: `git merge-tree` of this branch with sol1/review07-d7 is clean. compile.py gets one
    import and three lines in _ctx. _trade's keyword is `fill=None` (array); Sol1's
    exit_sig_available stays a separate keyword.
