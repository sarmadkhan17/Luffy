ATTRIBUTION_LIMITATIONS:
Read-only mode=ro connections plus query_only; short metadata queries and bounded indexed rowid inspections; no immutable=1 reads of live WAL stores. A few reads hit contention or their progress limit and were recorded; no unbounded fallback scans. Per-table B-tree allocation, historical full-table duplicate rates and exact per-thread cycle/background I/O remain unmeasured. Snapshot observations span less than one second each but are not cross-database transactions; brief read locks in DELETE-mode SQLite can contend with writers. Observer output and small report artifacts are excluded from storage totals.
Physical net allocated growth includes WAL high-water allocation, natural checkpoint/copy effects, rollback-journal presence and attention pruning. Committed page_count minus freelist_count measures database internal occupancy, not free filesystem bytes or per-table retained payload. A stable file can absorb new records in free pages. Process write_bytes counts block I/O (including overwritten/checkpointed/temporary pages), wchar counts syscall output, and cancelled_write_bytes is separately recorded. None is a retained-storage measure, and the counters must not be subtracted as though they were a storage ledger.
Shared system journal allocation is an upper-scope observation, not exclusive LUFFY attribution. Full existing syslog/journal footprints include other processes. Python syslog bytes are independently tallied; journal unit metadata establishes the eight monitoring producers. Detached prior-release private health files were not included in current-instance runtime evidence totals. Whole-host df changes include unrelated work and open/deleted/temp files, so the gap against tracked totals is not unexplained LUFFY storage.
