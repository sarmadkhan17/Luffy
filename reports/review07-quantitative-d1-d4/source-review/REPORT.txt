OD07_06_HISTORICAL_REPLAY:
Exact producer/reader contradiction:
  E = candle interval-open timestamp, D = timeframe duration, R = local receipt.
  FINAL at acquisition: E + D <= R.
  annotate: available_ms = observed_ms = R (publication clock remains unknown).
  default replay: retained FINAL and available_ms <= E + D,
                  plus observed_ms/available_ms <= overall query cut.
For normal truthful acquisitions these jointly require R = E + D. A receipt one
millisecond late fails. A multi-year backfill received today fails every past
close, and pre-provenance projections cannot supply missing clocks. Sources with
separate observed/available clocks must satisfy both at the real decision cut.
No amount of ordinary after-close accumulation fixes this exact equality.

Retained earlier real observation: 15 measured symbol/timeframe combinations had
zero replay-eligible bars despite substantial stored 4h and finer history. This
review reuses those preserved observations and reproduces the algebra using
synthetic receipts; it did not reopen production databases or evaluate real
protected prices.
