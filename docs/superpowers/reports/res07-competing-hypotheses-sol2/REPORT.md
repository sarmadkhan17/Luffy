## Actual gap and change

The normal retained Investigation Research Bank consumer already verifies the
source chain, freezes hypotheses, retains provenance and routes supported
experiment shapes through protected quantitative admission. However, its two
v1 templates ask different questions (price breakout expression and exact
volume path). They are not mutually competing predictions. Support/opposition
and untested causal authority are only implicit in nested source provenance.

`trader/cognition/predictive.py` now generates immutable v2 proposals with an
explicit canonical contract for observables, descriptive support, retained
opposition, competition, measurable test definition, UNKNOWN causality,
UNTESTED predictive edge and scheduling-only priority. The benefit and
no-benefit templates have the same predictor, target, universe, horizon,
metric and baseline, with complementary `> 0` and `<= 0` predictions. The
exact-volume-path question is also retained. New generation verifies the
retained source projection, refusing invented or rehashed altered context.

Legacy v1 reconstruction retains the original fields and hashes rather than
upgrading records during replay. SQLite immutable triggers and exact-payload
conflict checks remain in the existing bridge. New hypotheses and legacy
records are validated against their registered schema/template, so merely
rehashing a forged authority, causal verdict, opposition or test cannot grant
new meaning or quantitative admission.

## Proof mapping

- Supported source: normal `read_sources` -> verified `ResearchSource` ->
  `propose` -> immutable `bridge_hypotheses`; malformed source/projection tests
  refuse before proposals. Source Bank bytes remain unchanged.
- Explicit scope/lineage: frozen universe, horizon, context, source result,
  investigation/Bank IDs, source hash and canonical retained chain are bound
  into proposal identity.
- Competition: normal source consumption retains both complementary breakout
  hypotheses with a shared competition group and reciprocal transformations;
  their distinct IDs survive restart without regeneration or deletion.
- Honest evidence: descriptive support is labelled as such. Context opposition
  is retained rather than netted away. Causal claims stay UNKNOWN and predictive
  edge stays UNTESTED, even when the source's descriptive result is SUPPORTED.
- Admission: proposals are HYPOTHESIS/PROPOSAL_ONLY. Existing planner controls,
  ablations, power, dependence correction, protected referee and factory gate
  remain authoritative. Quantitative protocol fixtures exercise support,
  failure, insufficient data, crash recovery and shared spent-error-budget
  recovery; they are not real-market edge evidence.
- Replay: canonical JSON roundtrip, persisted restart identity, immutable
  triggers, template integrity, v1 exact field/hash replay and retained learning
  feedback replay are checked.
