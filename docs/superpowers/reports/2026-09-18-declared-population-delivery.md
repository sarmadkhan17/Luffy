## Implementation and review

The new `trader.observability.declared` scheduled worker reads the frozen declaration, independently of kernel trading selection and attention's first-16 cap. It attempts **every declared member** using public production futures 4h candles, with four concurrent requests, six-second total subprocess deadlines, 28 response bars per member and at most 64 declared members. Unsupported/oversized declarations refuse entirely; they are never truncated. Watchdog bounds the whole pass to 120 seconds plus five seconds for forced termination. No credentials, orders, strategy admission or risk decisions enter this path.

Each member has a timestamped availability/error receipt, including empty, stale, invalid and timeout results; all members remain in scan membership and get evaluator rows. Subsequent passes retry every member. Freshness is checked again at scan completion, including bar-boundary crossings. First observation time is the actual detached-response clock, never the bar close. The existing bounded Store preserves identical retained versions and appends changed versions; availability before collection and before retained lineage remains unknown. No historical observations were reconstructed.

`data/declared_population.enabled` routes the two scheduled observation consumers to `data/declared-population/attention.db`. Kernel capture/trading remains unchanged. The scheduled collector has its own completion-health schema, exact scan identity and read-back proof; this is not a fabricated continuously-live worker or a relaxation of kernel recovery health. A new pass publishes `collecting` before work, so a crash cannot leave an old success usable. Consumers refuse stale/incomplete/mismatched declarations without falling back to kernel selection. Frozen-window population exports include the complete per-symbol collection scope and receipts. Data errors are separate from successful completion of a collection attempt.

The source has deliberately declared-universe scope. Outside-universe older cases (including BZ/SAMSUNG) have no observation guarantee from this worker; retain their missing outcomes as supplemental evidence. Existing historical source-version gaps are not repaired. The old standalone `scripts.verify_memory_outcomes.py` reads only the kernel attention store; do not use its missing-version counts as a source-complete audit of new declared scans.
