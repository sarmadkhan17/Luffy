## Status definitions

**EVIDENCE_TO_MAP**: Existing requirement recorded; exact row-level evidence has not yet been linked. This is NOT a finding that the feature is missing, broken or reopened.

**OPEN**: A specific measured gap or needed proof is recorded; work is not automatically authorized.

**IN_PROGRESS**: The single currently authorized work package. Result pending; do not launch parallel packages.

**AWAITING_EVIDENCE**: Implementation or a fix may exist; the specified operational/reproduction/closure evidence is pending.

**AWAITING_OWNER**: A visual approval, scope/policy value or adoption decision is needed. Not permission to invent it.

**CLOSED**: Only this row's exact closing condition is reported satisfied at its recorded revision/scope. Later regressions need a new evidence-linked event.

**DEFERRED**: Outside current release scope by design; visible but not counted as a current mandatory defect.

**BLOCKED**: Cannot proceed under its contract; an exact dependency, external condition or safety fact must be recorded.

## Operating rules

**Authority**: SDD.md defines the target. STATE/NEXT remain the current summary and single selected work package. This is their detailed derived ledger, not another architecture authority.

**One data set**: Maintain this YAML as canonical detailed data. Dashboard Tracker reads validated YAML and NEXT. Markdown is optional generated reference. Historical XLSX is deprecated/non-authoritative and excluded from synchronization and adoption checks.

**Current work**: OBS-04 is terminal CLOSED with verified-exit observer retention and real fixture-timer proof. No successor is selected; OBS-02 is recommended only. PERF-01 and PERF-03 remain unchanged and BLOCKED. No runtime, provider or trading activation is authorized.

**Activation**: No item, green cell, spreadsheet formula or passing test activates trading, paid providers, new workers, a restart or a scope expansion.

**Intake**: A new finding needs ID, parent requirement, why, source or exact violated invariant, evidence/reproducer, impact, dependencies and closure condition. Unknown origin stays unknown.

**No moving goalposts**: Keep the acceptance version that the work started under. Change it only with an explicit reason/owner decision. A different requirement is a linked item, not a silent edit.

**Closure**: Link exact test/run/config/code identity; verify normal caller, relevant failure/restart cases and touched frozen invariants. Mark only the demonstrated proof level.

**Historical PASS**: Do not convert a package-level count into per-behaviour or runtime proof. Do not rerun old audits solely because an item is EVIDENCE_TO_MAP.

**Regression**: Preserve prior closure. Record a new event with reproducer, affected invariant and evidence for introducing revision; repair only the justified scope.

**GUI**: Track per-screen functional proof, runtime truth and owner visual approval separately. No agent may award the owner's visual approval.

**Blocked work**: Record internal dependency, policy, external evidence or owner gate distinctly. Do not simulate missing authority or fabricate retrospective evidence to close it.

**Observations**: Warm-up, no opportunity, no eligible rule, disabled paid provider and policy-blocked research can be valid outcomes. They cannot prove an unexercised capability.

**Metrics**: Summary counts are tracker workflow counts, NOT percent complete, weighted product progress or readiness. No fixed profitability/sample/retention thresholds are invented.

**Updates**: After each completed package: attach result, update affected IDs, add new issues, record history and select exactly one approved next ID in NEXT.

**Data**: Identical immutable payloads may share storage. Distinct observation times/identities remain. Backups are intentional redundancy; retention/deletion requires a proved dependency boundary.

Repository-control commits after that are documentation/tracker changes and must not be represented as observed runtime.

**Adoption**: Owner accepted representation and the read-only Tracker navigation. OPEN-09 closes through linked SDD/STATE/NEXT and explicit engineering ownership of YAML/optional Markdown synchronization. Product-wide EVIDENCE_TO_MAP does not prevent adoption.

**Safety exception**: An immediate observed safety incident may require containment under existing authority; record the issue and outcome promptly. A tracker queue never overrides safety.
